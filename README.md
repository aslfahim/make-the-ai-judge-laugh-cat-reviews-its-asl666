# Make the AI Judge Laugh: Cat Reviews Its Human

One funny single-line one-star review from a house cat about its human —
the vacuum cleaner edition. The cat gives one star because its human keeps
a loud roaring monster in the closet and unleashes it without warning, then
acts surprised when the cat hides under the bed.
